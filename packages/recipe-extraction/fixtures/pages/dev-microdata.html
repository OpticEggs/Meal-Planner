Invented Maple Oat Bars

By Jordan Fixture

Synthetic bars written for the benchmark.

Makes 16 bars

Prep: 20 minutes · Bake: 35 minutes · Total: 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup maple syrup
  • 1/3 cup melted coconut oil
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Heat the oven and line a square pan.
  2. Stir everything together in a bowl.
  3. Press into the pan and bake until golden at the edges.
  4. Cool completely before cutting into bars.
