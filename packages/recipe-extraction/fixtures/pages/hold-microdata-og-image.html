Invented Brown Butter Cookies

From Morgan Testcase

Makes 24 cookies · Prep 25 min · Bake 12 min

You will need

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, browned and cooled
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Beat the cooled brown butter with both sugars.
  2. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Stir in the flour and baking soda, then the chocolate chips.
  4. Chill the dough for at least an hour.
  5. Bake in balls on lined sheets until the edges set.
