# Disposition of R2's review of CONTRACT §13, the alias manifest and the 2C labels (coordinator, 2026-10-10)

R2 was blind to every engine. Each decision below is argued from the contract text. No frozen label changes.

| R2 finding | Decision |
|---|---|
| §13.1/§13.2 quantity vs the `N` flag | **Accepted.** For an undeclared unit or measure word the label is quantity, unit and package null, with the number and noun in `note` as written. Labels and engines now agree. The difference from the pre-fill fields of frozen holdout-v3 0075/0193 is stated in §13.1; those labels and scores are unchanged |

| §13.7 "role label" undefined | **Accepted.** It is defined as §7.10's unstated-amount phrases written as a label |
